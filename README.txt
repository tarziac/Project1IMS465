The mechanic i had pitched back in week 1 was the basic jumping mechanic in the game Jump King. 

It features a increasing float value for how long the player holds space to determine jump
height while locking them out of being able to move. 

When a player collides with a wall midair they also will bounce off of it.


I used Delta Time to track the increasing float value and make it follow how long has passed instead of just going off of frame rate.

I pretty much kept the exact same idea from idea 1.
I didn't really know how to make any of it but I got help from videos online and learned
a few things on what made it work.
